How to solve them fast

  1. Times before plus. Work out the multiplication part first, always.
  2. Start from the biggest chunk — it has the fewest options.
  3. Use the last digit as a filter: 7 × 3 ends in 1, so the rest must supply the rest.
  4. Odd or even? An odd total cannot come from two even parts.
  5. Remember each digit can only appear once. That kills a lot of near misses.
