What wins here

  1. Check the letter first, then the dots. Never both at once.
  2. Count dots in one glance — two plus one, not one-two-three.
  3. Most images are "no". Do not let a run of them make you careless.
  4. Keep a finger on each key so answering costs no movement.
  5. Blink between trials, never during one.
