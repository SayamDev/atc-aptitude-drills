How to play it

  1. Read the whole sentence before looking at the options — the ending usually decides the answer.
  2. For spelling, sound out the syllables one at a time; doubled letters are where the traps live.
  3. For vocabulary, decide if the word is positive or negative first. That alone kills two options.
  4. Eliminate before you guess. Wrong answers may cost you marks.
  5. Never spend more than about fifteen seconds on one item.
