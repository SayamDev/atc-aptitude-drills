How to attack one

  1. Trace the ball's route backwards from the black square first.
  2. Only touch blocks sitting on that route. Everything else is a distraction.
  3. Biggest blocker first — small pieces have more places to park.
  4. Everything slides until it hits something — the ball too. It only stops on the target if a piece, wall or board edge sits just beyond it.
  5. The red arrows show which sides the ball could arrive from right now. No arrows means you must first park a blocker behind the square.
  6. Tangled? Reset and replan. It is cheaper than untangling.
