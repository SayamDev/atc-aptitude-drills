How to count moving dots

  1. Hold your gaze on the centre. Don't let your eyes chase a dot.
  2. Count in threes, not ones — 3, 6, 9, 12.
  3. Sweep once, top band to bottom band, and commit.
  4. Never recount. A second pass counts a different arrangement.
  5. If it's crowded, count the gaps or the tight clusters instead.
