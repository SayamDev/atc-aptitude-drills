The three-step routine

  1. Read the GYRO. That's your heading, and it picks the aircraft — do this first, always.
  2. Read the RBI needle as an angle from the top. That's how far right of the nose the beacon lies.
  3. Add them. Heading + needle = the direction of the beacon from you.
  4. Flip it 180°. You sit on the opposite side of the beacon — that's the cell.
