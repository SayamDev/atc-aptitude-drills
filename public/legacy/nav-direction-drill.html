The one rule

  1. Watch the swing, not where the arrow ends up pointing.
  2. Clockwise = right, anticlockwise = left. True from any starting direction.
  3. Heading down the screen the car faces you, so its left is your right. The swing rule dodges that entirely.
  4. Same arrow twice = straight on. Exact opposites = turn back. Those two need no thinking.

Bear, turn, sharp

With eight directions the compass has eight points, so count how many notches the arrow moved. Each notch is 45°.

  1. 1 notch — bear. A gentle veer, like taking the left fork of a road.
  2. 2 notches — turn. The proper 90° turn at a junction.
  3. 3 notches — sharp. Doubling back on yourself at an angle.
  4. 4 notches — turn back. A U-turn; the arrows point exactly opposite.

Count the notches first, then decide the side: clockwise makes it a right, anticlockwise a left. So one notch clockwise is bear right, three anticlockwise is sharp left.
