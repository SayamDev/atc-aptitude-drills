What actually helps

  1. Rest a finger on each key before the pair appears. Most of your time is moving, not deciding.
  2. Scan position by position, left to right, both cards at once. Stop the moment you find a difference.
  3. "Same" always costs longer — you have to check every position. Expect it and don't panic.
  4. After a mistake, take the next one at normal speed. Chasing it causes the second error.
  5. Blink between trials, not during one.
