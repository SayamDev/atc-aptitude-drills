Run the checklist in this order

  1. Count. Total symbols, or how many of one shape.
  2. Compare the two examples — anything that differs between them is noise.
  3. Rows and columns. Is one line uniform, or all-different?
  4. Position. Centre, corners, diagonal.
  5. Symmetry. Mirror left-right, top-bottom, or rotated.
  6. Empty cells — they count as information too.
